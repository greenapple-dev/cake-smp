About Cake SMP

Who we are and what this server is actually about.

Cake SMP started as a small survival world for a handful of friends who wanted a vanilla server without the griefing, the pay-to-win ranks, or the noise. Season 3 keeps that same idea: build things, play fair, and stick around for the people, not the plugins.

Server Rules

Keep it fair so everyone can enjoy Season 3.

Discord Rules

  1. No disrespectful behavior towards other members.
  2. No toxicity or harassment towards other members.
  3. English only in #general, #media, and all other public channels.
  4. No self-promotion.
  5. No ragebaiting or provoking other members.
  6. No sexist, racist, or discriminatory content.
  7. Do not post content intended to offend, target, or seriously upset other members.
  8. No spamming in #general, #media, and all other public channels.

Minecraft Server Rules

  1. No hacking or cheating. This includes minimaps, X-ray, hacked clients, or any other unfair modifications.
  2. No lag machines.
  3. No mods giving an unfair advantage.
  4. No alternate accounts.
  5. No spawn / camp killing.
