Welcome to Cake SMP! This is your ultimate destination for pure, unadulterated vanilla Minecraft survival gameplay. Dive into a tight-knit community where every single block placed tells a story.

Our server averages around 10 active players online at a time. While we might be smaller in size compared to massive networks, our community is vibrant, friendly, and deeply creative. We focus on genuine player connections and epic long-term building projects.

To keep the gameplay dynamic and fun, we have custom rule sets: crystal PvP is completely disabled to ensure fair and balanced combat, but we've happily brought back string dupers and infinite trades for an extra layer of classic survival freedom!
